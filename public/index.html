⚡ What's Different?

A strategic twist on the classic game. Your oldest move vanishes after placing your 4th piece. Outsmart your opponent before your moves disappear!

×

🎮 How to Play

  • Battle on a 3×3 grid — classic arena, new rules.
  • Two players alternate turns — X goes first, then O.
  • You can only have 3 active moves on the board at a time.
  • Place a 4th piece? Your oldest move disappears instantly.
  • Watch for faded pieces — they're about to vanish!
  • Align 3 of your current marks in a row to claim victory.
  • In online mode, a 15-second timer keeps the action fast.
  • Run out of time? Your opponent wins by default.
