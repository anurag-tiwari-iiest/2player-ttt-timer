# Privacy Policy for CrazyToe

**Last Updated: January 26, 2026**

## Introduction

Welcome to CrazyToe ("we," "our," or "us"), developed by **AZ Develops**. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application ("App"). We are committed to transparency and protecting user privacy.

---

## Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or payment details.

However, the App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information as described below.

---

## Local Data Storage

The App stores limited information locally on your device to improve gameplay. This data does not leave your device and is not shared with us or third parties.

### Game Preferences

- Player names and symbols (for local gameplay)  
- Game settings (timer preferences, difficulty levels)  
- App preferences (theme, sound, and haptic settings)  

### Game Statistics

- Total games played  
- Wins, losses, and draws  
- Current and best win streaks  

This data can be deleted by:
- Uninstalling the App, or  
- Clearing App data via: **Settings → Apps → CrazyToe → Storage → Clear Data**

---

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob (Advertising)

The App uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including:

- Device information (device type, operating system)  
- Advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)  
- General location (derived from IP address, not precise location)  
- App usage and ad interaction data  

We do not have access to or control over the data collected by Google AdMob. This data is processed in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy:  
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized ads on your device:
- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization or Reset Advertising ID

---

## Online Gameplay

The App uses **Socket.IO** to enable online multiplayer functionality. During online gameplay, the following temporary data may be transmitted:

- Game moves and board state  
- Room IDs for matchmaking  
- Timer and turn data  

This data is used only to operate the game session and is not permanently stored on our servers.

---

## Legal Basis and User Consent (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we rely on your **consent** as the legal basis for processing personal data related to personalized advertising. You may manage your consent preferences through the App’s consent dialog (if shown) or your device’s advertising and privacy settings.

## Permissions

The App uses the following permissions:

- **INTERNET** – Required for loading ads and enabling online gameplay  
- **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE** – Required to check network connectivity before loading ads or connecting to game services  

These permissions are used solely for App functionality and not for direct data collection by us.

## Age Requirement (18+)

CrazyToe is intended for users **18 years of age and older**.

This age restriction exists **solely due to the presence of third-party advertisements (Google AdMob)**, which may display content not suitable for minors.

There is **no 18+ or restricted content within the App itself**. The gameplay and features are appropriate for all ages, but access is limited to adults to comply with advertising and platform policy requirements.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 18. If such data is discovered, it will be deleted promptly.

---

## Data Security

We do not store personal data on our servers. Any locally stored App data is protected by your device’s security features. We recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date.
